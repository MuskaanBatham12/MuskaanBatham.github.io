Projects

Vaidya AI

Vaidya AI is a healthcare technology platform designed to bring multiple healthcare services together in one system. The project focuses on improving access to emergency assistance, hospital resources and AI-assisted physiotherapy.

  • Hospital Resource Allocation: Helps identify and rank hospitals based on factors such as available resources, patient severity, distance and hospital load.
  • SOS Assistance: Provides an emergency workflow for patients requiring urgent healthcare support.
  • AI Physiotherapy: Uses pose analysis to assist users with exercise and posture monitoring and provides feedback during physiotherapy exercises.
  • Health Monitoring: Designed to integrate hardware such as ESP32 sensors for collecting health-related measurements.
  • Healthcare Services: The platform brings together features such as doctor consultation, hospital information, emergency support and patient-related data.
AI • Healthcare • Web • Hardware

Kisan Credit

Kisan Credit is a technology-based project focused on making credit-related information and services easier for farmers to understand and access. The idea is to simplify the interaction between farmers and financial resources through a user-friendly digital platform.

  • Simple Interface: Designed with accessibility in mind so that important information can be understood without unnecessary complexity.
  • Credit Information: Helps users understand available credit-related information and the requirements associated with it.
  • Farmer-focused Approach: The project focuses on presenting information in a way that is more useful for farmers rather than using a complicated financial interface.
  • Digital Access: Aims to reduce the difficulty of finding and understanding relevant financial information through a centralized platform.
  • Real-world Problem Solving: The project was developed with the goal of applying technology to a practical problem faced by users in the agricultural sector.
Web • Agriculture • FinTech
